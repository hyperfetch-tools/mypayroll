2. Top 4 Causes of Government Portal File Rejections

  • KWSP Contribution Month Mismatch: KWSP i-Akaun requires the Contribution Month to be 1 month ahead of the salary month (e.g., October salary = November contribution).
  • Excel Stripping Leading Zeros on Post-2000 MyKad: Staff born in 2000–2009 have NRICs starting with 00–09. Excel exports 020415145678 as 20415145678 (11 digits), causing immediate row rejection.
  • PERKESO ASSIST 244-Byte Integer Cents Rule: PERKESO ASSIST rejects decimals like 3500.00—all wages and contributions must be padded integer cents (00000000350000).
  • LHDN CP39 Fixed-Width 57/136-Byte Layout: Employer numbers must omit the letter E and Employee TINs must omit the IG prefix inside the 10-digit numeric column.
